### About Us
FosuSec is a cybersecurity lab initiated by students of Foshan University.
We conduct research and practice on information security, vulnerability discovery, CTF competitions, cryptography and secure tooling.
Team members collaborate to share technical knowledge, maintain open-source projects, organize learning notes and publish competition resources.

Our research areas include:
- Capture The Flag (Web, Pwn, Crypto, Reverse, Misc)
- Network protocol, internal network auditing and traffic analysis
- Cryptography and post-quantum cryptography
- Secure development with Python and Go
- Open-source security project maintenance

---

### Join Us
This team is open to enrolled students of Foshan University with a strong motivation for cybersecurity.
Prior experience in CTF, programming or cryptography is preferred.

To apply, contact the organization owner with your GitHub account for an invitation.
